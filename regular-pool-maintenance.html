Every visit

Each service includes

Whether it's a one-off clean or part of an ongoing schedule, every visit is completed to the same standard — the pool, the water and the equipment, all checked properly.

Filter pressure is recorded before and after every service, with backwashing or filter cleaning carried out whenever it's actually needed — decisions, not guesswork.

What's covered on the day

We come out, do the work, and leave you with a clear record of it.

  • Removal of leaves, debris and surface contaminants
  • Brushing of pool walls, steps, tile line and waterline
  • Vacuuming as required
  • Emptying of skimmer and pump baskets
  • Inspection and tidy-up of the equipment area
  • Checks of the pump, filter, visible pipework, valves and chlorinator or sanitising system
  • Professional water testing and chemical balancing
  • Chemistry report emailed after the service
  • Service notes and relevant before-and-after photos provided
