Why now

Summer doesn't wait for a quiet weekend

Heat, more swimmers and longer days all push a pool harder than any other season — and a few weeks of neglect shows up fast.

Chlorine burns off faster

Heat and UV strip sanitiser out of the water quicker, so a routine that worked in winter can fall behind.

Algae blooms in days, not weeks

Warm water is exactly what algae needs. A pool that looks fine on Friday can be green by Monday.

Small issues get expensive fast

A pump running rough or an overdue filter is a cheap fix now — and a costly one once it fails mid-summer.
