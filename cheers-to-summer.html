Get ready for summer

Everything your pool needs before the heat hits

From a quick tidy-up to a full equipment check — here's where most Perth pools need attention before summer really gets going.

Regular cleans

A scheduled clean keeps the water swim-ready through the busiest months, instead of catching up after a party or a heatwave.

Green pool prevention

Warm water is when algae takes hold fastest. A water test and a balance check now heads off a green pool later.

Equipment check

Pumps, filters and chlorinators work harder in summer. A quick check now catches the small issues before they become expensive ones.

Pool advice

Not sure what your pool's telling you? Get straight answers on water chemistry, filtration and cleaning frequency.

Why now

Summer doesn't wait for a quiet weekend

Heat, more swimmers and longer days all push a pool harder than any other season. A few weeks of neglect shows up fast — and costs more to fix than it would have to prevent.

Chlorine burns off faster

Heat and UV strip sanitiser out of the water quicker, so a routine that worked in winter can fall behind once the weather turns.

More swimmers, more demand

Sunscreen, sweat and extra bodies in the water push filtration and water balance harder every time the pool gets used.

Algae blooms in days, not weeks

Warm water is exactly what algae needs. A pool that looks fine on Friday can be green by Monday.

Small issues get expensive fast

A pump running rough or an overdue filter is a cheap fix in spring — and a costly one once it fails mid-summer.
