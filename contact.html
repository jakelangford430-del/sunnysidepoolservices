Working with Sunnyside

The practical questions

How jobs, access, payment and chemicals actually work. Anything not covered here, ask Daniel when you call.

Do you do one-off jobs, or is it regular servicing only?

Both. A lot of our work is ongoing scheduled servicing, but one-off jobs and one-off cleans are just as welcome — storm cleanups, property handovers, or a pool that's been left too long.

Do I need to be home?

Not usually. As long as we can get to the pool area we can do the job, and a lock box or similar arrangement works fine. Just let Daniel know how access works when you book.

How and when do I pay?

We'll issue an invoice for the work, payable within 7 days.

Are you insured?

Yes — Sunnyside is fully insured.

Do you supply the chemicals, or do I?

We do. You don't need to source or store anything; Daniel brings what your pool needs on the day.
